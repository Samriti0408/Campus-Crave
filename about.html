About Campus Crave

Campus Crave is a smart Campus Canteen Management website designed to simplify food ordering for students and staff. Instead of standing in long queues, users can browse the menu, add their favourite food to the cart, and place orders quickly.

Fresh Food

We provide delicious, fresh, and hygienic meals prepared daily for students.

Quick Service

Save your valuable time by ordering food online before reaching the canteen.

Easy Ordering

Order your meals in just a few clicks with a simple and user-friendly interface.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a faster, smarter, and more convenient food ordering experience for every student on campus while reducing waiting time and improving overall canteen management.
